Dungeon Crawl

Description

Dungeon Crawl is a 2D puzzle platformer game inspired by similar games such as Fireboy and Watergirl and It Takes Two. Our game features in-depth and difficult puzzles, a powerup system, and a split-screen camera. It is free to play on this website as of June 8th.

Playtesting Reports

Playtests were conducted in class and at home by friends. In-class playtests gave us feedback in a google form, and at-home playtests gave us direct verbal feedback. After our final playtest, our main changes were bugfixes, specifically removing powerups after a checkpoint, having UI show on screen properly, and having gates between levels work properly. As for recommendations for future expansions, we would plan to make more puzzles throughout the main map, add sound effects, and add more enemies.

Team Reflection

Our best accomplishment while creating our game would probably be our scripts. We have many different abilities and interactions that required complex programming, but we were able to complete everything modularly and with smooth performance. With a total of 35 distinct scripts, it was impressive how we were able to code everything within our time limit while maintaining performance.

If we had another 2 weeks to work on our game, our highest priority would be to expand the main map and add more puzzles. Second, we would add sound effects to enhance gameplay enjoyment. Third, we would introduce new enemy types and add mechanics for players to defeat them using either the environment or newly added attacks.
